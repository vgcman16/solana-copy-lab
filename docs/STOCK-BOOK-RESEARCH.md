# Stock PAPER Book Research Ledger

## Purpose

CopyLab does not treat an investing book as executable truth. User-provided sources may propose falsifiable hypotheses, but only causal, cost-adjusted PAPER evidence can evaluate them. Book-derived work is structurally unable to place orders, alter sizing, relax filters, promote a policy, or enable live execution.

Version: `stock-paper-book-research-v2`

Only public bibliographic metadata, registry IDs, page/chapter locators, and short paraphrased hypotheses are stored. Each dashboard `sha256` value is a deterministic identity digest derived from the committed registry ID, title, author, and format. It is not a checksum of any user's local PDF or EPUB. Book text and local source-file fingerprints are not copied into the application or sent to a model at runtime.

The public source identities, exact shadow-hypothesis wording and locators, and research version are sealed into a canonical shadow-manifest digest. That digest is part of every book-policy and policy-set identity, so an older score is hidden until the changed manifest is evaluated again.

Every source is labeled `CURATED`, `CAUTION`, or `QUARANTINED`. A quarantined source can appear only as provenance for an explicit exclusion; startup validation rejects any attempt to use it for an active or shadow hypothesis. The Bert Yates EPUB is quarantined because its package metadata names a stock book while every XHTML title names an unrelated trigger-point therapy book. *The Book of Questions* is quarantined because it is a personal-values book rather than a market source.

Source and hypothesis IDs must be unique. Production startup enforces one-to-one shadow-policy linkage, quarantine eligibility, public source-identity agreement, and the analysis-only flags. Persisted scores are accepted only when their learner version, dataset, checkpoint clock, current policy digest, and research-only contract all match.

## What is measured now

- Immutable pre-trade context and append-only simulated-order evidence.
- Same-clock SPY excess for realized event-backed trades.
- Fixed, cost-adjusted counterfactual challengers on causal outcomes.
- Hard eligibility evidence before relative signal ranking.
- `BOOK_DISCIPLINED_EXECUTION_PROXY`: two confirmations, minimum modeled dollar volume, controlled VWAP/5-minute extension, a tighter spread, and 25 bps additional stress cost.
- `BOOK_QUALITY_FIRST_LIQUIDITY_PROXY`: a stricter tradability proxy with 30 bps additional stress cost. It is explicitly not a measure of business quality or intrinsic value.
- `BOOK_MEAN_REVERSION_RECLAIM_PROXY`: a separately frozen liquid pullback/reclaim proxy with explicit spread, dollar-volume, VWAP-distance, short-horizon direction, and stress-cost constraints.

The three book-derived policies are frozen shadow policies. Their dataset and policy digests are persisted with the existing shadow-policy checkpoint. Paths may overlap and are never represented as independent portfolio trades.

## Blocked until trustworthy point-in-time data exists

- Free-cash-flow yield, ROIC, dilution, net debt, dividends, and historical valuation.
- Publisher identity, event time, novelty, ticker confidence, and independent news confirmation.
- Historical sector membership, adjusted sector ETF bars, and corporate-action-safe symbol history.
- Simultaneous sector/correlation concentration using aligned adjusted returns.
- Objective moving-average, crossover, breakout, and RSI ablations with causal warm-up history and multiple-testing controls.
- Causal breakout-quality features and false-break labels: frozen resistance formation, ATR-normalized candle geometry, opposing-wick ratio, same-clock relative volume, follow-through clock, and retest/failure outcomes.
- Opening-range and next-session breakout execution counterfactuals with auction, gap, stop/target ordering, partial-fill, and shared-capital realism.
- Prior-bar-only range-bound rejection versus unconditional boundary touch and the existing VWAP-reclaim proxy.
- Winner-only tranche addition and partial-exit/trailing-remainder lifecycle tests under an unchanged total exposure and risk cap.
- Corporate-action, overnight-gap, halt, opening-auction, and no-fill realism for stop simulation.
- Point-in-time specialist-universe comparisons with delisted-symbol controls.

Missing data is not estimated from current values or reconstructed with hindsight.

## Permanently excluded from intraday features

- Multi-year buy-and-hold, periodic-contribution, and rebalancing rules used as minute-level triggers.
- Averaging down or ignoring an intraday stop because a long-term thesis remains intact.
- Dated stock picks, leverage or margin shortcuts, rumors, subjective chart names, and stale broker claims.
- Penny-stock promotion, options payoff rules, forex systems, and crypto tactics imported into the US-stock lane.
- Personal values, hypothetical dilemmas, intuition, or self-rated risk appetite used as market features or autonomous sizing inputs.
- Anecdotes, author confidence, and performance promises.

## Interpretation rules

1. A positive shadow result is a hypothesis result, not a profit forecast.
2. Small samples and multiple challengers can create false winners.
3. Walk-forward breadth, costs, missing-data coverage, and drawdown remain mandatory context.
4. No automatic promotion path exists.
5. The lane remains PAPER-only.
